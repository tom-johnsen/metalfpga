Generate a wide integer type and helpers for the given bit width.

The macro emits a struct GpgaWide<width> with words 64-bit limbs and a family of helpers named gpga_wide_*_<width>.

Parameters
widthTotal bit width of the generated type.
wordsNumber of 64-bit limbs used to store the value.
last_maskBit mask applied to the most significant limb.
